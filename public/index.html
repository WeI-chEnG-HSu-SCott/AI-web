HTML-based network Insectary

Every web request is a subtle evidence of existence.

I am a bug breeder who runs this insectary. Like bending down to look closely at underground insects living in grass, this site gives audiences a way to observe beings that are hard to sense by turning completed local HTTP requests into live events.

Explore the experiment

Begin with the statement

What is seen?

Completed request, connection, and browser-declared metadata are recorded. The keeper recognises these small beings as present and consequential, and tends this place as an environment where many forms of digital life may gather.

What is protected?

Cookie, Authorization, and request-body content are not recorded. Common credential-like query values are redacted. The keeper is responsible for the safety and health of the beings that enter the farm.

How can you take part?

The farm exists first as a habitat for its inhabitants. People curious about these small beings are welcome to pause and observe, provided they do not disturb them.
