About the Insectary

The site focuses on the subtle presence within our network environment. Since the early twenty-first century, network automation has evolved alongside the Internet. Google search engines, web crawlers, spiders, and automated scripts now creep across the Internet everywhere. On June third, 2026, automated access to HTML (57.5%) on the Internet first exceeded that of real users. Yet people not only don't notice them but also ignore them, even though they surf the virtual world every day.

Like bending down to look closely at underground insects living in grass, this site lets audiences observe beings that are hard to sense. The site is hosted on a local computer. It records every non-human visitor and transforms the data into an artistic demonstration. The aim of Insectary is not only to reconsider the boundary between humans and machines but also to observe their familiar "automation" in the digital world.
