About the Inter-zap

In the endless wilderness, these tubes emit a 300–460 nm light—a siren song written into the very genes of insects.

Inter-zap focuses on the subtle presence within our network environment. Since the early twenty-first century, network automation has evolved alongside the Internet. So far, Google search engines, web crawlers, spiders, and automated scripts have spread across the Internet. On June third, 2026, automated access to HTML (57.5%) on the Internet first exceeded that of real users. The Internet environment has shifted from reduction of complexity to an emergent nature that more closely reflects our reality. Yet people not only don't notice them but also ignore them, even though they are part of the virtual world on which we surf every day.
